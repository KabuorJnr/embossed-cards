From blank card to their phone in a minute

  1. Choose a card

    Gold leaf, glitter, letterpress and die cut flowers that spill over the edge. Tilt your phone and the foil catches the light.

  2. Write inside it

    Your words appear in handwriting on the insert as you type. Pick casual, script or classic.

  3. Send the link

    They get a sealed envelope. One tap cracks the wax seal, and the card slides out for them to open.

There is no sign up and nothing to pay. The card's words are packed into the link you send, so we never see your message.
