Privacy policy

Last updated %%UPDATED%%

Embossed is built so that we never see the cards you make. This policy explains what that means in practice.

The short version

  • We have no accounts, no database and no server that receives your card.
  • Your card's words are packed into the link you send, after the # sign. Browsers do not send that part of a link to any server, so it never reaches us or our host.
  • We use no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. Fonts and code are served from our own site, not from third parties.
  • If you say yes, your unfinished card is kept in your own browser so it is still there when you come back. You can say no, and you can clear it at any time.

What is processed, and why

The card you write

The design, the words on the cover, your message, the name you write it to and how you sign it are encoded into the card link inside your browser. We do not receive or store them. Anyone who has the link can open the card and read it, so treat the link like a letter: share it only with the person it is for, and do not include passwords, ID numbers, bank details or other sensitive information.

Your unfinished card

Only if you choose "Keep my draft", your unfinished card is saved in your browser's local storage on your device. It is not sent anywhere. Choosing "Don't keep it" removes any saved draft.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. This is handled by GitHub under its own privacy statement. We do not receive these logs.

Sharing apps

When you send a card through WhatsApp or another app, the link travels through that app under its own terms and privacy policy.

Children

Embossed is not aimed at children. People under 13 should only use it with a parent or guardian's permission, and we ask everyone to confirm this before sending a card. Because we collect no personal data, we do not knowingly hold any information about children.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to be informed about how your data is used, to access it, to correct it, to have it deleted and to object to its use. Because we do not hold your data, most requests are answered by the tools in your own browser. You can still write to us at %%EMAIL%% and we will reply within 14 days. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

See Delete my data for how to remove everything Embossed may have kept on your device.

Changes

If we ever add features that change how data is handled, such as payments, we will update this policy before those features go live and show the date at the top of this page.
