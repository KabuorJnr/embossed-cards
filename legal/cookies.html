Cookie policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Embossed sets no cookies. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

What we keep, and only if you agree

We use your browser's local storage, which works much like a cookie but is never sent to a server. When you first visit, we ask whether we may keep your unfinished card. Both answers are equally easy to choose.

NameWhat it holdsHow long
embossed.storageYour answer to the question above, so we do not ask again.Until you clear it
embossed.draftYour unfinished card: design, words, names and handwriting. Saved only if you chose "Keep my draft".Until you clear it or choose "Don't keep it"

Change your mind

Use the button below to clear both items. We will ask again on your next visit.

You can also clear site data in your browser settings at any time.
