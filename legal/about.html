How it is made

Every design is drawn in code, so the artwork is original to Embossed. The fonts are open source under the SIL Open Font Licence and are served from this site. The designs draw on things we love from home: frangipani and hibiscus from the coast, the acacia at dusk, and the kanga with its saying printed along the hem.
