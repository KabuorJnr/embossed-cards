Accessibility

Last updated %%UPDATED%%

We want everyone to be able to make and open a card.

What we have done

  • Everything works with a keyboard. The card opens with Enter or Space, and design choices move with the arrow keys.
  • Screen readers hear the card's design and, once it is open, the full message.
  • If your device asks for reduced motion, the card stops swaying, the showcase does not play and the envelope opens without animation.
  • Text and controls meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, and every control is at least 44 pixels tall.
  • Decorative artwork is hidden from screen readers so it does not get in the way.

Known limits

The foil and glitter effects respond to tilt, which needs motion access on some phones. The card still works fully without it.
